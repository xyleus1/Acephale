# Results recorded November 22, 2026

This is a custom tau-compatible pilot, not an official full-benchmark score.
Training, replay, opportunity measurements, and the retained native cohort have
produced outputs. Scientific qualification remains unsuccessful; the repository
does not claim a validated end-to-end latency or goal-conditioning improvement.

## Retained native cohort

| Nominal arm | Scheduled slots | Reward-one successes | Execution errors | Step-limit outcomes | Known constraint-clean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Base actor | 32 | 28 | 4 | 0 | 21 |
| Neutral-memory prefetch | 32 | 27 | 4 | 1 | 21 |
| Acephale | 32 | 29 | 3 | 0 | 21 |

The denominator retains all 96 scheduled slots: 84 successes, 11 execution
errors, and one max-steps outcome. Nine error rows had no observed actor or
provider dispatch. They remain failures in the original analysis, not completed
evaluations or replacement candidates. Constraint audits are unknown for the
remaining four, five, and three slots respectively; unknown is not clean.

Both native services had a 5,400-second lifetime. The executed source schedules
shutdown at that limit, and their recorded aborted closures occur after about
90 minutes plus teardown. This explains the observed `Gateway closed` errors
more closely than the larger owner timeout; no timer-specific reason code was
logged. The failed rows and the original experiment denominator were preserved.

Every held-out prefetch checkpoint was rejected by the opportunity gate:
809 rejections, zero auxiliary-predictor dispatches. There were 1,213 actor
dispatches and 1,212 completions. Consequently these nominal arms executed the
actor-only path; their reward differences do not identify a goal-conditioning
effect. The 36 development slots similarly rejected all 247 prefetch
opportunities before retrieval or prediction.

All native comparative timing samples are invalid because the required host
guard was absent. No speedup is reported. The retained scheduler summary also
has missing telemetry for all 32 trials per arm; its known-zero totals must not
be read as complete measured zeros. Raw path events separately establish the
gate rejections.

## Training and remaining qualification failures

The two predictor seeds completed 204 optimizer updates each using 1,074
examples from 358 independent checkpoints. The actor remained frozen. No
discriminative tool-expert head was trained in this experiment.

Seed 29 serving qualification still fails the unchanged probability tolerance:
reference 0.23370635691404026 versus served 0.21206880435710532, an absolute
difference of 0.021637552556934947 against tolerance 0.01. The discrepancy was
observed on both L40S and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell; that does not establish a
hardware cause. Weights and acceptance tolerances were not changed to pass it.

Replay findings are reported separately from native task outcomes. Neither
teacher-forced token accuracy nor replay prediction loss is a substitute for
live task success, and replay timing is not live latency.
