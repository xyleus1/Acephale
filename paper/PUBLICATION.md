# Public paper edition v0.2.0

The November 23 edition reports the closed v2 development experiment, native
predictor-head comparison and checkpoint/runtime sensitivity. It retains the
negative result: no live prefetch benefit was established, and the unchanged
development gate prevented the conditional held-out phase.

The paper uses the typography, table conventions, figure palette and release
presentation of Scalar-Pi. Its scientific data, uncertainty estimates and recorded
failures are unchanged. Rebuilding the public PDF and figures does not dispatch
models, repeat trials or recompute statistical estimates.

- [Release and downloadable artifacts](https://github.com/xyleus1/Acephale/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
- [Editable source and reproduction](README.md)
- [Original 132-trial native pilot](corrective-study-20261122/README.md)

The release manifest records the public file identities and the hashes of their
original study sources. Public projections exclude private transcripts, provider
bodies, credentials, financial records and infrastructure identifiers. Earlier
versioned packages remain historical evidence.
